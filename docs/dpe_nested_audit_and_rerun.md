The primary selected-affine minus full-validation difference is −0.172 pp on

Cross-fit uniform differs from the 75-head bag by only approximately +0.07 pp
and +0.01 pp, respectively. These are observations about the implemented original
procedure, not proof that the broader method cannot help.

## Confirmed audit findings

1. **Nesting leakage.** Original `run_task` constructs OOF scores once and then
   calls `inner_cv_scores` on those fixed, label-dependent scores. A score excludes
   its own row but can depend on other labels in the inner evaluation fold.
   In split 0 / inner fold 0, all 959 calibrator-training scores come from heads
   trained on 183–203 of the 240 inner-held-out labels. The ancestry range over
   all split/inner-fold combinations is 173–207. This compromises model-selection
   CV; it is not test-label leakage. The direction and magnitude of its effect on
   the primary test difference require rerunning the corrected procedure.
2. **Coarse direct WGA threshold search.** Original offsets step by 0.05 in logit
   space. All 70 cells choose approximately −1.42e−14 (zero), and both direct-WGA
   arms exactly match their uniform controls' group accuracies, OA and WGA.
   Exact search improves fitted-validation WGA on five of seven historical held-out
   caches by 0.03–3.77 pp. Example: standard seed 3, 84.91% to 88.68%. These caches
   are distinct from the nested run's unavailable OOF predictions. They demonstrate
   search inadequacy, not corrected test improvements. This search is downstream
   of primary affine selection and cannot directly cause its negative result.
3. **Prediction-graph mismatch.** Original inner CV evaluates calibration of one
   OOF ensemble score, while cross-fit inference averages five separately calibrated
   scores. Foldwise bias can change score ordering and is not one threshold on the
   final uniform mean. Full-validation controls also transfer an OOF calibrator
   to a full-data model without separately evaluating that full procedure in CV.
4. **Selection objective mismatch, not a selector implementation error.** Every
   saved selection agrees with the declared rule. NLL selects average/0.01 in all
   70 affine cells. Relative to average/0.1, mean inner NLL decreases by 0.0617
   (standard ERM) and 0.0984 (ERM*), while mean inner WGA decreases by 0.195 and
   0.414 pp. The observed test penalty relative to the fixed arm is 0.338 and
   0.196 pp. The pooled affine arm shows the same NLL preference despite excluding
   the whole combiner pool from base-head fitting. This supports investigating an
   objective mismatch independently of the nesting problem.

The original executed source was downloaded and hash-verified. Audit evidence and

## Corrected implementation and validation

The [corrected experiment package](../experiments/dpe_nested_corrected/README.md)
rebuilds all base heads and calibrators inside inner training folds, evaluates
each deployed prediction graph separately, selects each family independently,
and applies exact scalar WGA thresholds after averaging. It retains the original
primary NLL rule, secondary WGA rule, four settings, fixed arm and baseline controls.
DFR preprocessing now stays inside CV and its solver RNG is fixed. New output
names, source/config/code/runtime fingerprints and stage checksums prevent silent
reuse of old results. Failures and incomplete runs are reported explicitly.

Ten regression tests and synthetic integration smoke tests for both real head
trainers passed. All 28 arms and checkpoint replay were exercised. Synthetic
settings were one head epoch and eight calibrator steps, rather than production
training, so they establish software execution only. The final embedded notebook
was extracted and tested; its embedded ZIP matches the separately supplied source
archive. The complete corrected Kaggle experiment is still pending.

## Research and submission implications

The current evidence is unfavorable to an improved-method claim. The audit leaves
the properly nested empirical question open; fixing our own code is not itself
an independent research contribution. Do not replace the declared primary rule or
select settings from the existing test table to manufacture a positive result.

Recommended next sequence:

1. Complete all corrected cells under the frozen protocol.
2. Analyze objective mismatch using balanced NLL, OA, every group accuracy and
   WGA together. Separate calibration, ensembling and threshold effects.
3. Add one independent replication, ideally a second compatible benchmark.
   Additional independent backbones improve precision, particularly for ERM*,
   but do not establish cross-dataset generality.
4. Frame the manuscript around the supported finding: an improved adapter if it
   robustly wins; a simplification study if thresholding matches it; or a careful
   study of selection objectives if NLL improves while WGA deteriorates.

A provisional framing is “When Calibration Fails Worst-Group Accuracy: An
Empirical Study of Frozen-Feature Adaptation,” conditional on corrected evidence.
Avoid a broad MLP/hyperparameter search before resolving the present mechanism.
Historical test inspection remains a limitation even after a technically clean
rerun. Do not claim reduced background reliance solely from improved WGA.

ACIIDS lists originality, technical depth, clarity and relevance in its
[submission guidance](https://aciids.pwr.edu.pl/2027/submit.html).
[TMLR acceptance criteria](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/acceptance-criteria.html) emphasize
claims supported by accurate, convincing evidence. These criteria support the
