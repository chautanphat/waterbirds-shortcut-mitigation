The contact sheet shows the original model crop, a mask overlay on the CUB cutout, and six natural recompositions. It includes small birds, thin structures and native crop truncation. Before inference the candidate backgrounds were screened for interpretable land/water scenes and visible bird/foreground objects. Edges and cropped anatomy still show occasional compositing artifacts. See the [preflight sheet](mask_contact_sheet.png).

The registered CUB cutouts and official masks were checked against the dataset manifest, with the same native threshold of 204/255 as the previous paired run. The chosen region retained native RGB. The compositor performed the shared premultiplied resize to 256 square, then used the existing POTER Runner to center crop to 224 and apply ImageNet normalization. No blur, rotation, jitter or crop randomness. The compositor verified that opaque foreground pixels and pixels outside mask support were copied exactly from their respective layers. Alpha, layer, mask, label and source ID were fixed over all 40 backgrounds per bird. The 6,400 prediction records contain 40 distinct model-view hashes per foreground. Metadata was used after scoring and for sampling, with only image tensors passed to the model.
