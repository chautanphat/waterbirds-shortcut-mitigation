No mask threshold, dilation, opacity hardening, blur, enhancement, JPEG re-encoding or learned segmentation. Lossless in-memory gray images were inferred.

Those soft masks are not hardened or dropped; RGB before blending stays identical, and soft regions blend by their supplied alpha. Alpha can be soft over broad interior regions as well as edges, so this fixed convention can reduce contrast there; no claim of all support pixels being opaque is made.
